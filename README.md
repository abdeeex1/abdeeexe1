### 🛠️ What I'm Building

**FIXGAD — Built for Morocco 🇲🇦**

FIXGAD is a map-first service marketplace designed to connect customers with trusted skilled professionals, known as *Hrayfis* (حرفيين).

The vision is to make finding, hiring, and working with local professionals easier through one platform.

With FIXGAD, customers can:
- 📍 Discover professionals through an interactive map.
- 📝 Create service requests called Missions.
- 💬 Receive and compare offers from professionals.
- ⭐ Explore profiles, experience, and reviews.
- 🤝 Connect with the right professional.

Professionals can create their own profiles, discover nearby opportunities, submit offers, and grow their businesses.

**Our ambition: Build something meaningful for all of Morocco, starting in Agadir.**
